Gobliins 2, the DOS floppy edition—on your phone.

Import your own game once, then play. Your files and saves stay in this browser. Goblins 3 is separate and unchanged.

1. Bring your floppy game

This first test supports the English US v1.03 release. Use Gobliins_2_floppy_ready.zip, prepared from your two floppy images, or a folder containing the assembled game files. Raw .7z archives and disk images are not supported by this importer. CD support comes later.

Checking local game storage…

2. Make yourself at home

  • On iPhone, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, then rotate to landscape.
  • Drag to move the pointer; tap to click. Two-finger tap is the right mouse button. A mouse and keyboard work too.
  • Move the pointer to the top edge to reveal the game's menu. Save and load there; saves are separate from Goblins 3.
  • Tap Sound if audio needs waking up. The small Menu button returns here without restarting the game.

Files are never sent to a server. Browser storage can still be cleared by the device or its settings; don't rely on private browsing to keep saves.
